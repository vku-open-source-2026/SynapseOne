container image at /usr/share/licenses/synapseone/. See LICENSE, NOTICE,
THIRD-PARTY.md, SOURCE-OFFER.md, inventory.json and the CycloneDX SBOM for the

The generated file THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.generated.txt contains the license texts

expression and source URL. This concise notice is not a substitute for those
upstream copyright notices.
